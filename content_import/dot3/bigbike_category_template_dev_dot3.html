QBalo thường và balo mô tô chuyên dụng khác nhau thế nào?

Balo mô tô có dây đai ngực + bụng giữ chặt khi chạy, lưng cứng hoặc đệm chống va đập, chất liệu chống nước hoặc có áo mưa kèm. Balo thường không có các tính năng này — rung lắc ở tốc độ cao, mưa là ướt hết đồ.

QNên chọn balo hay túi treo xe cho chuyến phượt dài ngày?

Đi dài ngày (3+ ngày) nên kết hợp cả hai — balo nhỏ 15–20L cho đồ cần lấy nhanh, túi treo xe 2 bên hông hoặc túi yên cho quần áo và đồ nặng. Chỉ dùng balo lớn một mình thì mỏi lưng và ảnh hưởng cân bằng xe.

QTúi bình xăng có tương thích với mọi dòng xe không?

Không — túi bình xăng dùng nam châm gắn vào nắp bình. Xe có nắp bình nhôm hoặc composite thì nam châm không bám. Rhinowalk và Givi có dòng dùng dây đai thay nam châm — phù hợp hơn cho xe không tương thích nam châm.

3 most common questions
QWhat is the difference between a regular and a motorcycle-specific backpack?

Motorcycle backpacks have chest and waist straps to stay secure at speed, a rigid back panel or impact padding, and waterproof material or an included rain cover. Regular backpacks have none of these — they shift at high speed and soak through in rain.

QBackpack or bike-mounted bags for a multi-day tour?

For 3+ day trips, combine both — a small 15–20L backpack for quick-access items, plus side panniers or a seat bag for clothes and heavier gear. Using only a large backpack causes back fatigue and affects bike handling.

QAre tank bags compatible with all bike models?

No — tank bags use magnets to attach to the fuel cap. Bikes with aluminium or composite tank covers will not hold magnets. Rhinowalk and Givi offer strap-mount alternatives suited for non-magnet-compatible bikes.

QHow is a Kewig mount different from standard mounts?

Kewig uses a one-handed twist lock and vibration-damping ball joint — the phone stays secure on rough roads and angle adjusts quickly without stopping. Standard mounts use spring clips — more vibration, and some cannot hold larger phones securely.

QWhat is the difference between Kewig M36 and M33?

Kewig M36 is for larger phones up to 7 inches with a wider clamp range. M33 suits phones between 5–6.5 inches, more compact overall. Both feature vibration-damping ball joints and one-handed lock.

Yes — Kewig produces mounts and adapters compatible with the GoPro standard (1/4 inch), DJI Osmo Action and Insta360. Ask in-store for the specific mount that suits your camera model.

3 most common questions
QWhat is the difference between rain gear and a waterproof touring jacket?

A touring jacket has integrated waterproofing — handles moderate rain. A separate rain suit handles heavy sustained rain and works over any jacket including mesh. The two complement each other, not replace each other.

QHow does Bigbike's own-brand base layer compare to Taichi?

Taichi is a Japanese brand — premium materials, higher price, includes cold-weather thermal options. Bigbike base layers are designed for Vietnam's tropical climate with strong moisture-wicking for summer heat, at significantly better value. For cold mountain touring, Taichi is worth the investment.

QIs a base layer necessary in Saigon's heat?

More useful than most people expect — moisture-wicking base layers make wearing protective gear significantly more comfortable in heat. Wearing gear directly on skin causes chafing on longer rides. Polyester mesh or Coolmax does not add meaningful warmth.
