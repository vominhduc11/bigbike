Q1 EN: What is the difference between protective gloves and regular gloves?

Q2 EN: CE Level 1 vs Level 2 for gloves — which to choose?

Q3 EN: Short-cuff or long-cuff — which is better for city riding?
A3 EN: Short-cuff is more practical — easier to put on and take off, cooler, well suited for Saigon city riding. Long-cuff tucks into the jacket sleeve, protecting against wind and wrist exposure in a fall — necessary for long-distance or cold-weather riding.
